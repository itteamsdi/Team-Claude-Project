How to use this review
One file passes between three people. Follow these six steps in order.
Golden rule: Everything lives inside the HTML file. Every time someone clicks ⬇ Export & Share, all data is baked into the downloaded file. Nothing is stored on a server. The file is the record.
1
Manager
📋 Manager sets up the file
Open in Chrome. On the Overview page, fill in the employee's name, job title, and date. Click ⬇ Export & Share to download the prepared file.
2
Manager → Employee
📨 Send to the employee
Email or Teams the SDI_121_Review_Name.html file. No account needed — they just open it in Chrome.
3
Employee
✏️ Employee completes their sections
Opens the file in Chrome and completes the blue Employee fields and emoji buttons. Clicks ⬇ Export & Share and sends back.
4
Manager
👔 Manager completes their sections
Open the returned file — employee responses are already inside. Add Manager notes, star ratings, and complete the KPI section.
5
Manager & Employee
📊 Review the summary together
Click View summary → in the sidebar for wellbeing indicators, section ratings, comparison, agreed actions and manager reflection.
6
Manager
💾 Save the final record
Click ⬇ Export & Share one final time. Rename with the date e.g. SDI_121_Review_Jane_June2026.html.
