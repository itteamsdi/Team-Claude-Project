Role Purpose

To drive SDI's commercial growth by hunting and winning new business, proactively generating, developing and closing new customers across awards, conferences and events, exams, membership and training, and then retaining and growing the accounts won, owning the revenue result for the area and protecting gross profit through pricing discipline and collaboration with the team. Alongside this, the role leads and develops the Customer Engagement team to consistently deliver SDI's brand promise of Surprise, Delight and Inspire. This is a strategic, revenue-owning leadership role reporting to the Chief Financial Officer. The Head of Customer Engagement is accountable for the area's commercial outcomes and for the performance and development of the team, and is expected to proactively hunt opportunities and shape those results, linking activity back to financial return, rather than respond to requests or carry out day-to-day training administration.

In doing so, the role advances SDI's mission as the authority on service desk best practice. Every sale connects customers to SDI's professional qualification standards, the Service Desk Analyst (SDA) and Service Desk Manager (SDM) Professional Standards (v9.0), and to SDI's Global Best Practice Standard for Service Desk (v9.0), against which service desks are certified. Commercial growth and the raising of professional standards across the industry go hand in hand.

What "Head of" Means

A "Head of" role is a leadership position accountable for a whole function or area of the business, not an individual contributor or purely administrative role. At SDI, a Head of:

  • Owns outcomes for their area: is accountable for the results, targets and overall performance of the function, not just their own tasks.
  • Leads people: sets direction, expectations and standards for a team, and develops the people in it.
  • Holds decision-making authority: makes and is trusted with decisions within their remit, and manages the associated budget and priorities.
  • Sets and drives the plan: translates SDI's strategy into goals and delivery for the area, and reports on progress.
  • Represents the function: acts as the senior point of contact and voice for the area, internally and externally.

In short, the title signals accountability for an area and its people and results, the "how" and "what" are set out in the priorities and responsibilities below.

Key Responsibilities

  • Engage and interact with all customers in a way that demonstrates the SDI brand, by phone, email, live chat, social media or face-to-face.
  • Proactively develop sales opportunities and follow up new prospects and marketing generated leads across all product areas, using Salesforce, Pardot or any other platform that supports opportunities.

Commercial Ownership and Initiative

A Head of is expected to anticipate and shape their area's commercial performance, not simply respond to requests. In practice this means:

  • Bring ideas, not just answers: proactively identify opportunities to grow revenue or margin and come with proposals and options, not questions.
  • Link everything to financial return: express proposals, decisions and initiatives in terms of their commercial impact (revenue, margin, cost saved or value created). If it can't be linked to a return, it is an activity, not yet a proposal.
  • Protect gross profit; discount as a last resort: gross profit is influenced, not directly controlled, so protect it through pricing discipline, product and channel mix, and collaboration with the wider team. Use discounting only as a solution of last resort; where a discount is unavoidable, offset it by selling more so the gross profit result is protected.
  • Think outside the box with a solutions-driven attitude: use creativity to solve commercial problems, turn obstacles into opportunities, and open new revenue, always tied back to the numbers.
  • Own the outcome: take accountability for the result of the area, anticipate rather than wait to be asked, and shape the plan proactively.

Authority and Decision Rights

  • Discounting: may approve discounts up to 5% of list price; anything beyond requires Chief Financial Officer approval. Discounting is a last resort and, where used, must be offset by additional volume.
  • Pricing: works within agreed SDI price lists; non-standard or bespoke pricing requires Chief Financial Officer sign-off.

Scope and Boundaries

Customer Engagement is a distinct function from Business Administration (Bethaney Moore, Head of Business Administration), which sits under the Chief Customer Success Officer. Although the two roles were historically in one line, they now operate in separate reporting lines, so the boundary is functional rather than managerial:

  • Head of Customer Engagement wins and owns the customer relationship, sets the commercial direction, and carries the revenue and team leadership accountability.
  • Head of Business Administration (within Customer Success) organises and runs the resulting courses, bookings, logistics, PeopleCert exams, records and invoicing administration, and will close web sales.

Where a task involves training, Customer Engagement is accountable for the sale and the relationship; Business Administration is accountable for delivery and administration. This role does not carry day-to-day training administration duties.

Alignment with SDI's Best Practice Standard

This role most directly reflects and promotes the following concepts of SDI's Global Best Practice Standard for Service Desk (v9.0):

  • Leadership (Concept 1): Service Desk influence (1.2), promoting the Service Desk (1.5), driving a culture of continual improvement and customer experience (1.7 to 1.8), and leadership development (1.10).
  • People Management (Concept 3): recruiting, developing and leading the team, including skills, career development, reward and team engagement.
  • Customer Experience (Concept 7): customer experience (7.1), customer expectations (7.4) and relationship management (7.6).
  • Professionalism and ethics (Concept 9): upholding professional and ethical standards in every customer interaction (9.4).

Reference documents: SDI Global Best Practice Standard for Service Desk (v9.0); SDI Service Desk Analyst (SDA) Professional Standard (v9.0); SDI Service Desk Manager (SDM) Professional Standard (v9.0).
